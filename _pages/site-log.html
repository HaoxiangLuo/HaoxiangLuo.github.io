LOG 002 · DESIGN NOTES

Giving every page the same visual language

As the site gained more content, today was a chance to gather all the design decisions made so far. The aim was not to make the pages busier, but to give them a shared order: quiet at first glance, clear in use, and free from decoration that interrupts reading.

Black, white, and gray remain the foundation. System blue is reserved for publication labels, links, and interaction feedback. Rounded corners, fine borders, soft shadows, and generous spacing form the visual structure. On the home page, the profile panel and main content create a measured contrast without relying on a harsh solid black. The profile appears only on the home page, leaving the other pages focused on their content.

The navigation stays close to the top, with Home, Publications, Study Notes, and Site Log distributed evenly. The language control is now a true two-option segmented control: the current language remains selected, while the other option can be clicked independently and opens the matching translation of the current page.

Publications uses separate columns for the book and journal articles. The book receives a larger natural-landscape cover, while journal articles use a denser two-column card grid. Labels such as SSCI Q1, EI (JA), CSSCI, CSSCI Extended, and AMI stay in a consistent position at the top of each card. Unranked journals show no label while preserving the same visual rhythm.

Study Notes provides three cards for Research Methods, Intelligent Communication, and Global Communication, together with entrances for uploading files and adding links. Site Log uses a timeline for written entries and also reads recent GitHub commits automatically, so smaller changes can be traced without becoming full articles.

  • Unified content width, spacing, corners, and type hierarchy across the site.
  • Completed bilingual navigation and a genuinely interactive language selector.
  • Established one coherent card system for publications, notes, and site logs.
  • Added both manual log writing and automatic update synchronization.
  • Refined responsive reading for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

LOG 001 · THE FIRST DAY

Building a place of my own

Today, this website began to look like a real place rather than an untouched template.

It started with the basic things: my name, portrait, university, and research interests. Some changes were small, but they still took time. A title appeared twice. A photograph hid behind the navigation bar. The bottom of a page stretched farther than expected. Each problem was fixed, one at a time.

Later, the pages found their own order. The home page became quieter. Publications were arranged into books and journal articles. Study Notes gained three spaces for research methods, intelligent communication, and global communication. Chinese and English could finally stand beside each other and lead to the right page.

By evening, the website was still not finished—and that felt right. It now has enough room for future papers, notes, and changes. Today was simply the day the door opened.

  • Established the bilingual home page and navigation.
  • Designed the profile, publication cards, and Study Notes hub.
  • Added publication labels, natural landscape covers, and responsive layouts.
