A daily-updated shortlist of United Nations internships, traineeships and fellowships for a researcher in communication, computational communication and digital governance. Twenty UN organisations are read from their own career sites; every opening is then scored out of 100 on direction, function, topics, eligibility, working mode and organisation, and anything below 60 is never stored — the aim is to miss an irrelevant post rather than to admit finance, HR or engineering. Beside them sit PhD exchange, visiting and joint-training announcements from journalism and communication departments at QS top-50 universities. Nothing collected is ever thrown away: each day is filed under its own date and a later run never overwrites an earlier one.

Entries are indexed automatically from the official career pages of the organisations listed and from the department sites of the universities listed. Titles and links belong to the original publishers; deadlines, stipends and eligibility are read from the official notice and are subject to it — a field the notice does not state is shown as not stated rather than filled in. Inclusion does not imply endorsement. The match score is this site's own reading of the notice, not the organisation's opinion. Chinese summaries, labels and match reasons are generated by fixed templates; job titles are left in the publisher's wording until a translation provider fills them in.
