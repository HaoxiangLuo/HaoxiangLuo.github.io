Entries are indexed automatically from the official career pages of the organisations listed and from the department sites of the universities listed. Titles and links belong to the original publishers; deadlines, stipends and eligibility are read from the official notice and are subject to it — a field the notice does not state is shown as not stated rather than filled in. Opportunities past a known deadline are hidden; some sources do not provide deadlines, so please check the official notice. Inclusion does not imply endorsement. The match score is this site's own reading of the notice, not the organisation's opinion. Chinese summaries, labels and match reasons are generated by fixed templates; job titles are left in the publisher's wording until a translation provider fills them in.
